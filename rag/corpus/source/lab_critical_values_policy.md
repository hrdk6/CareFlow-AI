# LABORATORY CRITICAL VALUES POLICY

Document code: CF-LAB-02. Version 3. Owner: Clinical Laboratory.

SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT. Written for the CareFlow AI portfolio project; fictional policy.

## 1. Purpose

Critical values are results that may indicate an immediately life-threatening condition. This policy ensures that they reach a responsible clinician quickly.

## 2. Critical Value List

The following results are critical values at CareFlow General Hospital: glucose below 54 mg/dL or above 400 mg/dL; potassium below 3.0 mmol/L or above 6.0 mmol/L; sodium below 125 mmol/L or above 155 mmol/L; INR above 4.5; hemoglobin below 7.0 g/dL; white blood cell count below 2.0 or above 30 x10^9/L; creatinine above 4.0 mg/dL; eGFR below 15 mL/min/1.73m2.

## 3. Notification

The laboratory telephones the responsible clinician or the nurse in charge within 30 minutes of verifying a critical value. The receiver must read back the patient's two identifiers, the test and the value. If the responsible clinician cannot be reached within 30 minutes, the laboratory escalates to the on-call senior doctor.

## 4. Documentation

The clinician documents the notification time, the action taken and the plan in the medical record within 1 hour.

## 5. Repeat Testing

A first-time critical value that is clinically unexpected should be repeated on a new sample when doing so will not delay treatment.
